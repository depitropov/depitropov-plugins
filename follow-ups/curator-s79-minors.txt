Follow-ups from the S79 refactor of the Curator (navigation and curation, October 2026)

The task reviews and the final whole-branch review of the S79 plan
(docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-curator-navigation-curation.md) found these minor points.
Each one was parked on purpose: none of them blocks a run. Each item says what we saw, why it
can wait, and what to do.

orchestra
---------

1. A guide path may name a folder.
   What we saw: `file()` in plugins/orchestra/lib/check.js uses `fs.existsSync`, so a guide
   value such as "guides" or "." passes the check.
   Why it waits: the stage fails loudly when it tries to read a folder.
   What to do: use `fs.statSync(...).isFile()` in a try/catch, and add "guides" to the bad
   values in test/orchestra/check.test.js.

2. No check that a guide path stays inside the plugin after a symlink.
   Why it waits: plugin authors are trusted, and the limit is known.
   What to do: resolve the real path and compare it with the plugin folder, if a plugin ever
   ships symlinks.

3. The java guide's rules are not tested.
   What we saw: test/orc-curator/guides.test.js checks only that
   plugins/orc-java-stack/guides/code.md has no frontmatter and no harness words.
   Why it waits: the move from skill to file was a pure rename.
   What to do: assert two or three of its rules, as the Curator guides do.

4. The Codex marketplace has no allow list.
   What we saw: .claude-plugin/marketplace.json has `allowCrossMarketplaceDependenciesOn`;
   .agents/plugins/marketplace.json has none.
   Why it waits: we do not know if Codex has an equivalent. Plan 5 (Codex) is paused (S71).
   What to do: check it when plan 5 starts.

5. DESIGN §12 names a script that does not exist.
   What we saw: the layout lists `scripts/gen-manifests.js`. It was wrong before S79.
   What to do: remove the line, or write the script.

orc-curator: curate and its procedures
--------------------------------------

6. curate Step 12 contradicts Step 7 on the glossary.
   What we saw: Step 12 says to discard in GLOSSARY.md "as in Step 7"; Step 7 keeps the
   glossary at a landing and restores only docs/curator/.
   Why it waits: Step 2 stops curate when the glossary has uncommitted changes, so a diff run
   starts with a clean glossary.
   What to do: say in Step 12 which glossary edits a failed run discards.

7. The soft-row challenge also runs for a bug report.
   What we saw: curate Step 8 shows the touched `soft:` decisions before it sorts the change,
   so it also asks "still true?" when the change is a bug.
   What to do: say "before you write a `Dn` or a `Qn`".

8. A landing commits all uncommitted glossary edits.
   What we saw: at a landing, curate cannot tell the burst's glossary edits from other edits
   the user made, so it commits both.
   Why it waits: they are glossary edits the user made, and the summary shows the commit.
   What to do: show the glossary diff before the commit and let the user exclude a term.

9. A stopped landing leaves copied research notes behind.
   What we saw: the discard restores tracked files from the last commit; a research note that
   the landing copied into docs/curator/research/ is a new file and stays.
   What to do: remove the files the landing created when it stops.

10. The diff path does not apply the retire rule to a TECH change.
    What we saw: the words path retires the `## Evaluated` rows that depend on a changed
    dimension; the diff path does not.
    Why it waits: Step 6 already passes a live `Cn` that the shipped code contradicts to the
    sweep.
    What to do: apply the retire rule when a diff changes a TECH row.

11. The first landing on a repo with old ADRs creates many `hard:` decisions.
    What we saw: every ADR in docs/adr/ that no `Dn` links lands as its own `hard:` `Dn`,
    also an ADR written before the Curator or outside a burst.
    Why it waits: curate shows every marker before the commit.
    What to do: land only the ADRs that are new since the burst started, or let the words path
    link an ADR that the user names.

12. Weak tests for curate.
    What we saw: several assertions in test/orc-curator/curate.test.js match single words
    ("once", "no `Dn`", "diagnosing-bugs"). The reconcile branch ("discard nothing, name the
    files") has no test.
    What to do: slice each step and assert inside it, as the setup test does for its Step 3.

13. The direction test misses two wrong versions.
    What we saw: test/orc-curator/direction.test.js checks the changed-dimension strings one
    by one, and forbids only `orc-curator:setup`, so a bare "run setup" would pass.
    What to do: assert the branch and the nudge in the same verdict row.

14. Long lines and wording in the procedures.
    What we saw: very long unwrapped lines in procedures/backlog.md and procedures/direction.md;
    backlog Step 3 says "not saved in git yet" only to avoid the word "commit".
    What to do: wrap the lines; say "not yet in a commit" and exempt that phrase in the
    procedure test.

19. The setup description does not say that a set-up project is redirected.

20. One long line in setup Step 3 ("no step of the present").
    What to do: wrap it and reword it.
